          How to play

          1. Every day, the whole world gets the same riddle. It points to one place on Earth.
          2. Read the clue. Stuck? Reveal more clues, but each one lowers your points.
          3. Tap the map to drop your pin, then hit Guess. You get one shot.
          4. The closer you are, the more points (up to 5,000). Then see where everyone else guessed.
