          How to play

          1. Every day, the whole world gets the same 5 riddles. Each one points to a real place from a famous book or movie.
          2. Read the clue. Stuck? Use the spyglass for a hint: the first one shows the book or movie title and a new clue. Each hint lowers your points for that round.
          3. Tap the map to mark the spot, then hit Mark the spot. One shot per place.
          4. Each day climbs from easy to hard: 1 easy riddle (up to 600 points), 2 medium (900 each) and 2 hard (1,300 each). 5,000 a day in all.
          5. The closer you are, the more points. Be quick too: answer within 20 seconds for the full speed bonus.

          The daily game is free, always. No sign-up.
