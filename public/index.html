          New place every day at 00:00 UTC. Your rough location (city level) is used for the player map. No sign-up, no tracking cookies.

          How to play

          1. Every day, the whole world gets the same 5 riddles. Each one points to a place on Earth.
          2. Read the clue. Stuck? Reveal up to 3 clues, but each one lowers your points for that round.
          3. Tap the map to drop your pin, then hit Pin it!. One shot per place.
          4. The closer you are, the more points: up to 1,000 a round, 5,000 a day. Then see where everyone else guessed.
